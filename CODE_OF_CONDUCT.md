# Community code of conduct

Everyone contributing to this project, asking for help, reviewing changes or reporting issues is expected to:

- Be respectful and discuss technical ideas with specific evidence.
- Welcome newcomers and explain reproduction steps clearly.
- Respect others' privacy; never publish credentials, private correspondence or identifying information without permission.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory language and personal attacks.
- Accept a maintainer's decision to close repetitive, abusive or unrelated discussions.

Maintainers apply these expectations consistently. Depending on the behavior, they may request an edit, remove content, warn a participant or restrict participation. A participant may ask for reconsideration with relevant evidence.

Report conduct concerns through a private contact channel published on the [maintainer's profile](https://github.com/SongYuanKun). If no private channel is available, open an issue requesting a private contact without naming participants or describing the incident. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
